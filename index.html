Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to upload my files to use these tools?

No. Most tools (image compressor, converter, resizer, image-to-PDF, passport photo, PDF compressor, Word/Excel converters) run 100% inside your browser. Your files never leave your device. Only AI-powered tools (background remover, image enhancer, video converter) send your file to our server, where it is processed in RAM and deleted immediately after the result is returned to you.

Are these tools completely free?

Yes. All 15 tools are 100% free to use with no account, no sign-up, and no hidden fees. There are no per-session file limits for client-side tools.

How does the AI background remover work?

The background remover uses the U2Net deep learning model via rembg. Your image is sent securely to our server, the AI automatically detects and removes the background, and a transparent PNG is returned to your browser. The file is permanently deleted from the server immediately after download.

What formats do the document converters support?

We offer 3 separate converters: Word Converter (DOCX ↔ PDF), Excel Converter (XLSX ↔ PDF), and Excel to Word (XLSX ↔ DOCX). All processing is done client-side in your browser - no upload required.

What is the maximum file size allowed?

For AI-powered tools, the maximum image size is 10MB and maximum dimensions are 4000x4000 pixels. Client-side tools such as the image compressor and converter are limited only by your device's available memory.
