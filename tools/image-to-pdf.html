Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert multiple images to one PDF?

Select multiple image files (JPG, PNG, WebP), reorder them by drag and drop, then click Generate PDF. A single PDF containing all images is created in your browser and downloaded instantly.

Is this tool completely free?

Yes, 100% free with no limits, no sign-up, and no watermarks on the output PDF.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The entire conversion runs in your browser using jsPDF. Your images never leave your device.
