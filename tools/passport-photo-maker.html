Frequently Asked Questions

What are the USA passport photo requirements?

USA passport photos must be 2x2 inches (51x51mm), with a plain white or off-white background. The face must be centered and occupy 70–80% of the frame. Eyes must be open and looking directly at the camera.

Does this tool upload my photo to a server?

No. All cropping and print sheet generation runs entirely in your browser. Your photos never leave your device.

What countries are supported?

USA, UK, India, EU/Schengen, Pakistan, Canada and Australia. The tool automatically applies the correct dimensions and generates a 4x6 inch print sheet for your selected country.
