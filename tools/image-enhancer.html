Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI image enhancer work?

Your image is sent to our server where RealESRGAN — a state-of-the-art deep learning model — upscales it up to 4x resolution, restoring fine details and removing blur. The enhanced image is returned and your original is deleted immediately.

What is the maximum image size?

Maximum 10MB file size and 4000x4000 pixels per image. The AI will upscale your image by 4x, so a 1000x1000 image becomes 4000x4000.

What is the difference between the AI models?

RealESRGAN x4 is best for photos and real-world images. The Anime model is optimized for illustrations and cartoon art. GFPGAN specializes in restoring and enhancing human faces in portraits.
