Frequently Asked Questions

What are the correct Instagram image dimensions?

Instagram square posts are 1080×1080px. Portrait posts are 1080×1350px. Landscape posts are 1080×566px. Stories and Reels are 1080×1920px. Our tool includes presets for all these formats.

What size should a YouTube thumbnail be?

YouTube recommends 1280×720 pixels (16:9 aspect ratio) for thumbnails. The minimum width is 640px. Our YouTube Thumbnail preset automatically resizes your image to the perfect dimensions.

Does resizing reduce image quality?

When scaling down, our tool uses high-quality Lanczos resampling via the Canvas API, which minimizes quality loss. When scaling up, some quality reduction is unavoidable but we use bicubic interpolation for the best result.
