Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to upload images to compress them?

No. All compression happens locally in your web browser using JavaScript. Your files never leave your computer or get uploaded to any server.

What image formats are supported?

We support JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, and BMP formats. You can process a mix of these formats in a single batch.

Will image quality suffer during compression?

You control quality with the slider. For most web uses, 80% quality is indistinguishable from the original while reducing file size significantly.

Is there a limit on how many images I can compress?

No artificial limits. You can process as many images as your browser's memory can handle.
