Frequently Asked Questions

How does client-side PDF compression work?

Using the pdf-lib library, we parse the PDF binary in your browser, remove redundant metadata, unused objects, and optimize the cross-reference table. This reduces file size without any quality loss.

How much will my PDF be reduced?

Results vary by PDF type. Documents with lots of metadata and unused objects can see 20–60% reduction. Image-heavy PDFs see less reduction since images are not re-compressed in client-side mode.

Will the compressed PDF look different?

No. The visual content of the PDF is completely unchanged. Only structural overhead and metadata are removed, which are invisible to viewers.
