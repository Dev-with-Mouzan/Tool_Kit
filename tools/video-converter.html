Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert MP4 to AVI online?

Drop your MP4 file, select AVI as the output format, then click convert. The video is processed entirely in your browser using WebAssembly. The process typically takes 30–120 seconds depending on file size.

What formats are supported?

Input: MP4, AVI, MOV, MKV, WebM, FLV and most common video formats. Output: MP4, AVI, MOV, MKV, and high-quality GIF with palette optimization.

Is my video kept private?

Yes! Your video is processed entirely in your browser. It never leaves your device. This means complete privacy - we can't see your files at all.
