Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI background remover work?

Your image is processed entirely in your browser using WebAssembly and TensorFlow.js. The U2Net deep learning model runs locally, removing the background and returning a transparent PNG.

Is my photo saved on your servers?

No! Your photos never leave your device. All processing happens locally in your browser using AI models running on WebAssembly. Complete privacy guaranteed.

Can I remove backgrounds from multiple images at once?

Yes. Select multiple files and the queue engine processes them one by one automatically. You can download them individually or all at once as a ZIP file.

What is the maximum file size?

Maximum file size is 10MB and maximum dimensions are 4000x4000 pixels per image.
